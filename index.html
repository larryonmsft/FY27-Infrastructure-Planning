FY27 Mid-Atlantic Infrastructure Strategy Plan

Infrastructure Solution Engineer | Public Sector (EDU, SLG, AMC)

Executive Summary: The FY27 strategy focuses on accelerating Azure adoption across Mid-Atlantic public sector accounts by combining executive seminars, technical workshops, and repeatable hands-on labs. The plan aligns vertical business priorities with modernization motions that move customers from discovery to pilot and into funded implementation. It emphasizes Azure Landing Zones, migration and modernization, resiliency, and secure AI-ready platforms to drive measurable workload expansion. Quarterly flagship events and monthly targeted engagements are designed to increase qualified pipeline, executive sponsorship, and cross-workload adoption.

State & Local Government (SLG)

Top Business Drivers

Citizen service continuity, regulatory compliance, infrastructure resiliency, and modernization of mission-critical legacy applications.

Complex procurement cycles, constrained staffing, siloed operations, and strict governance requirements across agencies.

What Customers Want

Reference architectures that satisfy compliance controls, faster disaster recovery postures, and practical migration paths that reduce risk.
